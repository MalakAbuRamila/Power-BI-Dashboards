# Amazon Sales Dashboard 📊

## Overview
This in an interactive Power BI dashboard which analyzes Amazon sales data to provide insights into product pricing,
discounts, and customer ratings. The dashboard enables exploring key trends, identify top-performing categories,
and understand the relationship between price, discounts, and ratings.

## Key Insights
- The dataset contains **1,349 unique products**, with an average rating of **4.1** and an average discount of **48%**.
- Products in the **mid-price range** tend to maintain higher ratings compared to very high-priced products.
- Most products cluster in the 4.0–4.5 rating range, reflecting strong customer satisfaction.
- Ratings slightly decrease as prices rise, suggesting expensive items don’t always deliver better customer experiences.
- Top 10 categories combine high ratings with strong discounts, making them key drivers of sales.
## Tools Used
- Power BI
- CSV dataset
- DAX measures

## Dashboard Content
**Slicers:** Category, Rating Count, Discounted Price. Discount Tier.<br>
**KPI's:** Average Discounted Price, Average Discount %, Average Rating, Unique Products.<br>
**Visuals:**<br>
1. Top 10 Product Categories by Average Rating & Discount Percentage(Stacked Column Chart)
2. Product Rating vs Actual Price (Scatter Plot)
3. Average Discount Percentage Across Price Ranges (Clustered Column Chart)
4. Top 5 Product Categories by Average discounted Price(Clustered Bar Chart)
