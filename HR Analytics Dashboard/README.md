# HR Analytics Dashboard 📊

## Overview
This project is a Power BI dashboard that provides clear insights into workforce demographics and attrition trends. 
It tracks key metrics such as attrition by gender, age group, education, and years at the company, helping HR managers
identify patterns and take action. Interactive visuals and filters make it easy to explore the data and support 
data-driven decisions on retention and recruitment.

## Key Insights
- Overall attrition rate: **16%**
- Average employee age: **37 years**
- Highest attrition in **26–35 age group**
- **Life Sciences (38%)** and **Medical (25%)** backgrounds show the highest attrition
- Early attrition peaks in the **first year of employment**
## Tools Used
- Power BI
- CSV dataset
- DAX measures

## Dashboard Content
**Slicer** for Departments.<br>
**KPI's:** Count of Employees, Attrition, Attrition Rate, Average Age, Average Salary and Years at Company.<br>
**Visuals:**
1. Attrition by Gender (Stacked Bar Chart)
2. Attrition by Education (Donut Chart)
3. Number of Employees by Age (Clustered Column Chart)
4. Job Satisfaction by Job Role (Matrix)
5. Attrition by Years at Company (Area Chart)
6. Attrition Rate by Gender for Different Age Groups (Donut Charts)<br>
